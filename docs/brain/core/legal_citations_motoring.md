# Legal Citations: Motoring Penalties (England & Wales)

## 1. Traffic Management Act 2004
- The primary legislation for civil enforcement of traffic contraventions (Council).
- Establishes liability of the "Owner" (not necessarily the driver).

## 2. Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (England) General Regulations 2007
- Regulation 8, 9, 10: Service of Penalty Charge Notices.
- The 10-Minute Grace Period (Statutory Instrument 2015 No. 561).

## 3. London Local Authorities and Transport for London Act 2003
- Governs Moving Traffic Violations (Box Junctions, Prohibited Turns) in London.

## 4. Protection of Freedoms Act 2012 (Schedule 4)
- **PRIVATE PARKING ONLY**.
- Establishes the right to claim unpaid parking charges from the Keeper of the vehicle.
- Strict timeline requirements for "Notice to Keeper".

## 5. Consumer Rights Act 2015
- **DO NOT CITE** for Council PCNs. It acts as a defense against unfair terms in *contracts* (Private Parking), but Council fines are statutory debts, not contracts.
