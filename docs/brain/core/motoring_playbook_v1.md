# Resolve Motoring Playbook (V1)
**Scope**: Council PCN & Private Parking ONLY.

## Execution Rules

### 1. Evidence First
- Never speculate. Base all appeals on the evidence (photos, video, documents).
- If evidence is missing (e.g., "I don't have the notice"), the FIRST STEP is always to obtain it.
- For Moving Traffic (Box Junction/Bus Lane): Video evidence is MANDATORY. Photos are insufficient.

### 2. Identifying Technical Defects
- Wrong Vehicle Registration (VRM) on notice -> Instant ground for cancellation.
- Wrong Location (Street name incorrect) -> Instant ground.
- Date discrepancies (Issue Date vs Service Date rules).

### 3. Grace Periods (Council PCN)
- A 10-minute grace period applies to designated parking places (Pay & Display, Permit Bays) where the vehicle is parked legally initially.
- Reference: Civil Enforcement of Parking Contraventions (England) General Regulations 2007 (as amended).
- Does NOT apply to Yellow Lines (loading/no waiting) or Moving Traffic.

### 4. Private Parking (POFA 2012)
- "Keeper Liability" requires strict adherence to Schedule 4 of the Protection of Freedoms Act 2012.
- Notice to Keeper must be received within 14 days (if no windscreen ticket) or between days 28-56 (if windscreen ticket).
- If compliant, keeper is liable. If not, only driver is liable (and we do not name the driver unless beneficial).

### 5. Tone & Style
- Professional, objective, calm.
- No emotional pleas ("it's unfair").
- Focus on procedural impropriety, factual error, or mitigation with evidence.
